# Code of conduct

## Expected behaviour

Everyone who takes part in this project:

- treats every participant with respect, regardless of background, identity or experience;
- keeps criticism specific, evidence-based and directed at the work, not the person;
- reports measurements honestly — invalid runs, failed tests, negative results and open problems
  are stated plainly and never dropped, hidden or inflated;
- does not present the instrument, its results or its safe-state logic as certified or suitable
  for controlling physical machinery.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sustained disruption, publishing another person's
private information, and knowingly misrepresenting measurements, methods or prior work.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to protoscience@anulum.li. The owner handles reports in confidence.
Responses range from a private warning to permanent exclusion from the project. People who report
in good faith are not subject to retaliation.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, reviews, correspondence about the project and any public
communication made on its behalf.
